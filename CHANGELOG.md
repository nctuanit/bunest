## [Unreleased] / 0.2.0 Complete 53-Package Ecosystem, Service-Layer AOP & Performance Upgrade

Major release completing **two landmark milestones** across **53 workspace packages (1,468 source files, 249,405 physical LOC)** with **100% ADR-adjusted NestJS v12 parity**, **0 new external runtime dependencies** (**48/53** packages zero-dep; strictly 4 unique external npm runtime deps across the monorepo), **0 RxJS**, **0 runtime/type/DAG circular imports**, **6,344 pass / 0 fail** tests (`29,804` assertions across `865` files), and verified zero hot-path regression (`bun run bench`: **34,648 req/s** static GET = **98.7%**, **33,968 req/s** param GET + DI = **99.4%**, **22,583 req/s** JSON POST = **84.3%**, **12,986 req/s** validated POST = **101.3%** of raw `Bun.serve`, `0.3688 ms` cold bootstrap, **1,542,353 ops/s** hot singleton DI):

- **Milestone 1 — Complete 53-Package Official NestJS v12 Ecosystem:**
  - **Group 1 (11 Core & DX packages):** **610/610 ADR (100.0%)** direct exports (`610/613` raw) and **2,476/2,476 ADR (100.0%)** public members (including **218/218** `@nestjs/common@12.5.1` and **54/54** `@nestjs/core@12.5.1` with Bun-native `AbstractHttpAdapter` per [ADR-013](./docs/adr/ADR-013.md)).
  - **Group 2 (11 Ecosystem & Data packages):** **475/474 ADR (100.2%)** (`475/488` strict) across 9 NestJS-paired packages plus Bun-native `@bunest/sqlite` (**68** exports) and `@bunest/sql` (**95** exports).
  - **Tier-1 & Tier-2 Official NestJS v12 Ecosystem (23 new packages):** **1,311/1,311 (100.0%)** root exports and **5,922/5,922 (100.0%)** public members (plus 100% parity across all multi-subpath exports `.`, `./testing`, `./postgres`, `./mysql`, `./workflows`, `./cqrs`, `./core`).
  - **Drop-In `@nestjs/*` Migration Facades (8 new packages):** `@bunest/jwt`, `@bunest/throttler`, `@bunest/passport`, `@bunest/serve-static`, `@bunest/terminus`, `@bunest/devtools-integration`, `@bunest/bullmq`, and `@bunest/prisma`.
- **Milestone 2 — Service-Layer AOP Method Aspect Architecture & Declarative Decorators ([ADR-014](./docs/adr/ADR-014.md)):**
  - Zero-overhead `DIContainer` `MethodAspectWeaver` (`createMethodAspectDecorator`, O(1) `WeakMap` presence cache, sync preservation, all DI scopes), `@UseMiddleware`, `@InheritMethodEnhancers`, `@Transactional`, Spring Data-style compiled-once `@SqliteRepository`/`@SqlRepository` + `JpaRepository`/`SqlCrudRepository` + `@Query`/`@Modifying`/`@SqlParam` + derived finders, `@Cacheable`/`@CacheEvict`/`@CachePut`, `@Span`/`@Trace`/`@Timed`/`@Counted`, `@EmitEvent`/`@EventPayload`, `@Auth`/`@Protected`/`SecurityModule.forRoot`, `@InjectConfig`/`@ConfigValue`, `@WithLock`, `@WithCls`, and service-level `@Retry`/`@CircuitBreaker`/`@Timeout`/`@Bulkhead`/`@Fallback`.

### Added — Decorator-First Architecture (Waves 1–3, [ADR-014](./docs/adr/ADR-014.md))
- **Pillar 1 — Self-Registering `DecoratorRegistry`, `@AutoModule` & Pre-Indexed Controllers (`@bunest/common`, `@bunest/core`, `@bunest/http`):**
  - Added `Symbol.for`-backed `DecoratorRegistry` (`registerController`, `registerInjectable`, `registerEntity`, `register(kind, cls, meta?)`, `getControllers`, `getInjectables`, `getEntities`, `getByKind<T>`, `getTargetsByKind`, `markInternal`, `snapshot`, `restore`, `clear`) automatically populated by `@Controller`, `@Injectable`, `@Entity`, `@SqliteRepository`, and `@SqlRepository`, with internal framework providers excluded via `markInternal()`.
  - Added `@AutoModule(metadata?)`, `ModuleMetadata.autoDiscover` (`boolean | AutoDiscoverOptions`), `ModuleScanner` auto-discovery expansion (preserving explicit `@Module` apps untouched), and `BunestFactory.createAuto(options?: BunestCreateAutoOptions)`.
  - Added decorator-time `WeakMap`-cached controller pre-indexing (`PrecompiledControllerDescriptor`, `PrecompiledRouteDescriptor`, `precompileController`, `getPrecompiledController`, `invalidateControllerCache`) with automatic cache invalidation on `MetadataRegistry` writes, consumed directly by `@bunest/http`'s `scanControllers()`.
- **Pillar 2 — Unified `@Entity` Single Source of Truth (`@bunest/common`, `@bunest/sqlite`, `@bunest/sql`, `@bunest/validation`, `@bunest/swagger`):**
  - Added unified entity schema decorators (`@Entity`, `@PrimaryKey`, `@PrimaryGeneratedColumn`, `@Column` with `design:type` auto-inference, `@Index`, `@Unique`, `@CreateDateColumn`, `@UpdateDateColumn`, `getEntitySchema`, `hasEntitySchema`, `getEntityColumns`, `getEntityIndexes`), re-exported by `@bunest/sqlite` and `@bunest/sql`.
  - Added dialect-safe `generateCreateTableSql(entityClass, dialect)` (`sqlite`/`postgres`/`mysql` with strict `assertSafeSqlIdentifier` checks), `SqliteModule`/`SqlModule` `entities`, `autoSync: true` (`syncEntities()`), and `autoRegisterRepositories: true`, plus full entity-aware auto-mapping/hydration/serialization (`boolean`, `json`, `datetime`, `transformer`, UUID PKs, auto-timestamps) in `@SqliteRepository(Entity)` and `@SqlRepository(Entity)`.
  - Added `validateEntity()` + idempotent `ValidationPipe` hook in `@bunest/validation` enforcing `@Entity`/`@Column` constraints (`whitelist`, `forbidNonWhitelisted`, `transform`, inheritance, skipping omitted generated PKs/defaults/timestamps, merging cleanly with `class-validator`).
  - Upgraded `@bunest/swagger` `SchemaExplorer` & `SwaggerModule.createDocument()` to auto-generate OpenAPI 3.1 schemas from `@Entity` without repeating `@ApiProperty()`, expand whole-entity `@Body`/`@Query`/`@Param`, and auto-infer default `200`/`201` `$ref` response schemas from `design:returntype` (safely ignoring `Promise`, `Object`, `Function`, `Array`, primitives, and undecorated classes).
- **Pillar 3 — Proxy-Safe & Factory-Safe `weaveProxyMethodAspects` (`@bunest/core`, `@bunest/sqlite`, `@bunest/sql`):**
  - Exported `weaveProxyMethodAspects()`, `weaveMethodExecutor()`, `AspectResolver`, and `collectClassAspectTokens()` in `@bunest/core`; updated `DIContainer` to weave `useFactory`-created instances and `Proxy` repositories automatically whenever `provider.token` or `created.constructor` has method/class aspects.
  - Updated `compileSqliteRepository` and `compileSqlRepository` to copy prototype method metadata via `MetadataRegistry.copyMetadata()` onto bound CRUD and `@Query`/`@SqlQuery` functions so AOP decorators (`@Transactional`, `@Cacheable`, `@Span`, `@Retry`, `@WithLock`, `@EmitEvent`) execute across CRUD, `@Query`, concrete, and derived methods.
- **Refactored Showcase App & Example:**
  - `apps/playground`: Refactored to full Decorator-First stack (`@Entity("todos")` driving SQLite `autoSync: true`, `ValidationPipe`, and OpenAPI 3.1 `/docs` + `design:returntype`; `@Span`-woven auto-registered `TodoRepository`; `@Span` + `@Transactional` `TodosService`; unified `ObservabilityModule.forRoot()` + `DevtoolsModule.forRoot()`; `@Param("id", ParseIntPipe)`).
  - `examples/auth-jwt-sqlite`: Decorated `UserEntity` and `TaskEntity` with `@Entity` + `@PrimaryGeneratedColumn` + `@Column` + `@CreateDateColumn` so `@SqliteRepository(UserEntity)` and `@SqliteRepository(TaskEntity)` derive table/column/JSON/timestamp mappings automatically while keeping `AUTH_SQLITE_MIGRATIONS` & `appliedMigrations` intact.

### Fixed — Pre-Beta Audit Gates (P0)
- **Swagger Bridge Delegation (`@bunest/swagger`)**: `SwaggerModule.setup()` now wraps the adapter's existing `CoreHttpBridge` (via `BunHttpAdapter.getBridge()`) instead of overwriting it, forwarding all 6 `HttpRuntimeBridge` methods and full `(enhancer, requestContainer, kind, controllerToken)` arguments to preserve module-local controller enhancers, kind-specific `TestingModule` overrides, and `@Catch()` mappings.
- **Zero Circular Imports (`@bunest/core`)**: Extracted `LifecycleManagerLike` in `core-types.interface.ts`, eliminating the single remaining type-only circular import (`0` runtime + `0` type-only + `0` cross-package DAG cycles across all 22 packages).
- **Public `DiscoveryService` & `MetadataScanner` (`@bunest/core`)**: Auto-registered built-in discovery singletons (`DiscoveryService`, `MetadataScanner`, `DiscoveryModule`), eliminating private `(moduleRef as any).host` casts across companion packages (`@bunest/graphql`, `@bunest/microservices`, `@bunest/observability`, `@bunest/cqrs`, `@bunest/events`, `@bunest/scheduler`, `@bunest/websockets`).
- **Redis RPC Reply Isolation (`@bunest/microservices`)**: Replaced shared `${prefix}:${pattern}.reply` channel with isolated per-client `${prefix}:reply:${clientId}` reply inboxes (`replyTo`).

### Performance — HTTP & Validation Hot Path (P0)
- **Single-pass JSON Body Parsing (`@bunest/http`)**: Added `Content-Length` fast-path (`req.text()`/`req.bytes()`), 1-pass prototype-pollution scanning (including `\u` escape detection) stamping unforgeable `BODY_SANITIZED_SYMBOL`, lazy `RequestContext`/`HttpResponseContext` allocations, and `executeFastSingletonRoute` fast-path for singleton routes.
- **Zero-Double-Walk `ValidationPipe` (`@bunest/validation`)**: Skips redundant recursive `findDangerousKeys()` walks when `BODY_SANITIZED_SYMBOL === true` (while retaining full checks on direct/unsanitized calls), adds plain-object fast-path skipping 17 `instanceof` checks, skips `Buffer` metatypes for `@RawBody()`, and pre-normalizes/freezes options once in constructor.
